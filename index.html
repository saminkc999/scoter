The finishing line

Five stages.
Zero shortcuts.

A finish is only as good as the prep beneath it. Every job moves through a controlled, inspected sequence before it leaves the floor.

01

Strip & Inspect

Disassembly, old-coat removal and a documented condition check of every part.

02

Blast & Prep

Abrasive blasting and chemical clean to bare, keyed metal; phosphate treatment.

03

Coat & Spray

Primer, powder or wet paint applied in a filtered booth to a controlled thickness.

04

Cure

Oven cure at exact temperature and dwell time for full cross-link and hardness.

05

QC & Finish

Film-thickness gauging, adhesion check, reassembly and final detail before handover.
